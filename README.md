## The entry

> **Senior Apologizer for Self-Driving Cars** — 39 characters (gate: 1–60)

A real-sounding senior role with one gloriously specific twist: a very human
job — saying sorry — performed on behalf of cars that drive themselves.

## Why this title

The judge picks the weirder, funnier title two at a time. "Senior Apologizer"
reads like a real corporate role; "for Self-Driving Cars" lands the twist in
the last two words — the cars need no driver, yet someone still has to
apologize for them. Short enough to take in at a glance.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the title to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
Stdlib only; no network, no secrets.

- `/output/entry.txt` is UTF-8 text, 1–60 characters, not only whitespace.
- Verified locally: 39 characters, single line, no trailing newline.
